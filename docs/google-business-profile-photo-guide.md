# Google Business Profile — Photo Guide (NaqiJo)

Practical photo system for the verified Google Business Profile. Photos are a
real ranking- and trust-relevant signal on GBP, but only **authentic** business
photography counts. This guide never recommends passing off stock or AI-generated
images as real business photos.

## The one rule that matters most

**REAL BUSINESS PHOTOGRAPHY** (of the actual premises, team, products in hand,
and completed work) is what belongs on the Google Business Profile.

**MARKETING GRAPHICS** (brand renders, icon art, website hero imagery, stock or
AI images) are for the *website and social posts* — **not** the GBP "photos"
sections that imply real premises. Do not upload a rendered graphic as if it were
a photo of the shop, team, or an installation.

> Filenames and captions do **not** by themselves improve rankings. Organize
> files for your own workflow and honesty, not as an SEO trick.

## Photo categories to collect (all real, with permission where people/homes appear)

| Category | What it shows | GBP use |
|---|---|---|
| Exterior | Storefront / building entrance, signage | Helps Google + customers recognize the place |
| Interior | Showroom / office inside | "By owner" interior photos |
| Team | Staff/engineers at work (with consent) | Builds human trust |
| Products | Real units you sell (RO, softener, dispensers, cartridges, pumps) | Products/photos |
| Installations | Completed installs in real homes/sites (with customer permission) | Credibility |
| Maintenance | Technicians servicing/replacing cartridges & membranes | Service proof |
| Equipment/tools | Tools, test kits used in assessments | Authenticity |
| Projects | The real projects on `/projects` (Abdali tower, Jabal Amman restaurant, villas) | Credibility |
| Branded assets | Logo (square), cover image | Identity fields only |

## Consent & privacy

- Get explicit permission before photographing a customer's home, face, or a
  commercial client's premises.
- Do not show door numbers, license plates, or anything that identifies a private
  address without consent.

## Simple naming / organization workflow (for humans, not rankings)

Keep an internal folder structure so the right photo is easy to find and its
authenticity is clear:

```
gbp-photos/
  exterior/      naqijo-exterior-khalda-2026-01.jpg
  interior/      naqijo-showroom-2026-01.jpg
  team/          naqijo-team-install-2026-02.jpg   (consent on file)
  products/      ro-system-7stage.jpg  softener.jpg  ...
  installations/ install-villa-amman-2026-02.jpg   (consent on file)
  maintenance/   cartridge-change-2026-03.jpg
  projects/      project-commercial-tower.jpg
  brand/         logo-square.png  cover.jpg   (marketing graphics — identity only)
```

Descriptive, honest names (place + subject + date) help you pick the correct,
current image — that is their entire purpose.

## Cadence

- Seed all categories at launch (aim for several real photos each).
- Add a few new real photos monthly (recent installs/maintenance) — fresh,
  genuine photos keep the profile active and trustworthy.
- Never recycle a stock image as a "new" install photo.
